Why it exists

Every assistant forgets the work you already did.

Cortex builds a private adaptation layer from connected services, local app integrations, chats, email, notes, writing, decisions, and messages. It shows what the model covers, what still needs signal, and lets approved AI tools retrieve scoped memory instead of guessing.

The product loop

Connect, review, ask, trust.

The first release focuses on the habits that make a personal model trustworthy: connect sources, approve useful signals, ask with citations, and let AI tools access only what you allow.

01

Connect

Connect supported services and local apps for chats, email, notes, messages, docs, writing, bookmarks, calendars, contacts, and work tools.

02

Review

Approve useful signals, archive noise, and keep the memory layers trustworthy.

03

Ask

Ask Cortex with cited memory, or use a supported local AI tool with the context you approved.

04

Trust

Keep reads, saves, exports, repair actions, and source permissions visible and under your control.
