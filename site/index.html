Why it exists

Every assistant forgets the work you already did.

Cortex builds a private adaptation layer from connected local tools and notes first. It shows what the model covers, what still needs review, and lets approved AI tools retrieve scoped memory instead of guessing.

The product loop

Connect, review, ask, control.

The first release focuses on the habits that make a personal model trustworthy: connect MCP or local notes, approve useful signals, ask with citations, and let AI tools access only what you allow.

01

Connect

Connect a local MCP AI tool or local notes folder once. Broader service connectors stay out of the primary flow until they truly sync.

02

Review

Approve useful signals, archive noise, and keep the memory layers trustworthy.

03

Ask

Ask Cortex with cited memory, or use a supported local AI tool with the context you approved.

04

Control

Keep reads, saves, exports, repair actions, and source permissions visible and under your control.

Connections & Privacy

Private by default, useful on purpose.

Cortex stores memory on the user's Mac, shows what is covered, and lets users choose what connected AI tools can read, save, export, or repair.

On your Mac

Readable memory records and a rebuildable search index live on disk.

Review first

New captures can wait in the inbox until the user approves them.

AI tool permissions

Reading, saving, exporting, and repair actions are controlled separately.

Reliability report

Health checks, backup recency, and repair actions are visible in the app.
