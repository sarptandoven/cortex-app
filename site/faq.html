FAQ & support

Short answers to the questions we hear most. Still stuck? Email support@trydoppl.com. See also the Privacy page and the Terms of Service.

macOS won't let me open the app

  • If the app is notarized (the normal download), just double-click Cortex.app — macOS opens it without a warning.
  • If you get a "cannot be opened" / "unidentified developer" warning (for example on an early build), right-click (or Control-click) Cortex.app → Open → Open. You only need to do this once.
  • On macOS 15 (Sequoia) and macOS 26, if the right-click trick is blocked, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to the message about Cortex, and click "Open Anyway." Then open the app again.
  • This is macOS Gatekeeper, not a Cortex bug. Always download Cortex from the official site and verify the checksum if you want to be sure of the file.

Is my data private? Is it encrypted?

  • Local memory (the default): the shipped beta requires a one-time account sign-in, but ingestion, retrieval, and your memory vault stay on your Mac unless you enable sync.
  • Cortex Cloud (optional): your data is stored in an isolated per-account store and is encrypted at rest with a key unique to your account. This is not end-to-end or "zero-knowledge" encryption: Cortex servers can read your memory in order to build embeddings, retrieve, and serve cited context to your AI tools. That is what makes the cloud features work.
  • Full detail — including deletion by crypto-erasure and what connectors read — is on the Privacy page.

How do I connect the app to my account?

  • Open the app's Settings → "Cortex Cloud" section.
  • Enter the hosted URL (for example https://api.trydoppl.com).
  • Sign in with your email and password, or use "Sign in with browser" for Google/GitHub. The "Create an account" link opens the signup page.
  • The app stores your session securely in the macOS Keychain and refreshes it automatically. Power users can still paste a raw endpoint and token in the manual fields.

How do I reset my password or verify my email?

  • Verify your email: after signing up you get a verification email — click the link to activate your account. If it did not arrive, check spam, then request a new one from the sign-in page. Some email is required before you can store connector credentials.
  • Reset your password: use the "Forgot password" link on the sign-in page. We email a single-use reset link that expires shortly. Setting a new password signs out your other sessions.
  • If a link expired, just request a fresh one.

How do I export or delete my data?

  • Export: you can export your memory at any time; the export is meant to cover everything associated with your account.
  • Delete: deleting your account destroys your per-user key (crypto-erasure), which permanently renders your encrypted credentials — including in any backup — unreadable, and removes your derived data. This cannot be undone, so export first if you want a copy.
  • For a formal data-rights request, email privacy@trydoppl.com.

A connector says it needs to reconnect — what do I do?

  • This usually means the third-party service (e.g. Gmail, GitHub, Slack, Notion, Calendar) expired or revoked the access token — it is normal and expected periodically.
  • Open Settings → the connector and click Reconnect, then re-authorize in the browser window that opens.
  • Connectors are read-only, so reconnecting only restores read access; it does not change anything in the connected account.

Does it work offline?

  • After the required sign-in, local ingestion and retrieval work with the network off. Signing in again, syncing, hosted retrieval, and connector authorization need a connection.
  • Cortex Cloud features (syncing, hosted retrieval) need a connection, since they run against the hosted backend.

How do I update the app?

  • Cortex checks for updates and links you to the current download. It does not replace the running app automatically.
  • You can also download the latest version from the download section and replace the app in your Applications folder.

How do I contact support?

Email support@trydoppl.com. For privacy or data-rights requests use privacy@trydoppl.com. Please do not send raw notes, chats, memory records, prompts, exports, API keys, or tokens by email — describe the problem instead.
