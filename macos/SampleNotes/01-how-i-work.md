# How I Work

I prefer concise, direct writing over long-winded prose.
I always batch meetings on Tuesdays, and I'd rather keep the rest of the week open for deep work.
I default to shipping a small thing every day rather than one big launch.
My go-to for planning is a single page, never a deck.
I generally reply to email in one pass, late in the afternoon.
When it comes to tools, I stick with plain Markdown for every note I keep.
