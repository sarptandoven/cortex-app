# Meetings I Avoid

I dislike long status meetings; they drain the whole afternoon.
I avoid ceremonial intros at the top of every call, they waste ten minutes.
I hate recurring meetings that could have been a shared doc.
My pet peeve is a meeting with no agenda and no owner.
I do not schedule anything before 10am, that time is for writing.
Marcus Feld feels the same way, so our syncs on Project Harbor stay to fifteen minutes.
When Dana Kim runs the infrastructure review, she keeps it tight, which I appreciate.
