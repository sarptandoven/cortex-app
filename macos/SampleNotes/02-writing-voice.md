# My Writing Voice

My writing voice is warm but precise, and I keep sentence length short.
My writing style leans plain: verbs over adjectives, no throat-clearing.
When I draft, the tone I want is a smart friend explaining a hard idea over coffee.
I usually write in the first person and read every line out loud before I send it.
I dislike buzzwords and I avoid corporate phrasing entirely.
Priya Rao reviews my longer essays; she has a great ear for rhythm and cadence.
This is the voice I want across Cortex, our docs, and every note about writing.
