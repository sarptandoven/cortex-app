CORTEX — STRATEGY (current, consolidated)
Cortex is your Digital Self: a private, self-owned model of how you think, decide,
and write — that any AI can become, and that works for you with receipts you control.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
0. THE ONE LINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
"Your AI is you, working — and you can prove and control everything it did."

Cortex learns you from the tools you already use, keeps that model of you on your
own machine, and lends it — cited and permissioned — to every AI tool, so they
draft and act the way you would. You always press send.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHERE WE ARE (honest)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Built and real: a local-first macOS app + local backend; a layered memory of you
(facts, events, style, decisions, preferences, dislikes, procedures) with sources,
time-validity, and citations; 13 read-only connectors (Obsidian, Gmail, GitHub,
Slack, Notion, Drive, Calendar, etc.); an MCP output layer so Claude/Cursor/ChatGPT
can query it; a permission + audit spine (scoped tokens, capability flags that
default OFF, an event ledger); and a multi-tenant hosted plane. ~578 backend tests.

The gap between what we SAY and what we DO:
  - Our "semantic memory" is currently a keyword-hash trick, not real meaning —
    real semantics only turn on with a cloud API key, which breaks the privacy
    story. (This is the #1 thing to fix; see the First Bet.)
  - Connectors only READ. There is no "act" surface yet — no way for the self to
    draft or do anything in the outside world.

So: a strong engine and a fuzzy product. The plan below makes the memory real,
makes the value visible, and turns it into a self that does work — safely.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. THE BIG IDEA — from "memory" to "a Digital Self"
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
We were thinking too small ("the best memory layer for AI"). Memory is the FUEL,
not the product. The product is a working model of you that can ACT on your behalf.

Three levels, in order:
  - PRODUCT (now): AGENTIC — the self does real work (starting with drafts), earned
    step by step through measured fidelity + your approval + a receipt.
  - WEDGE / MOAT: SOVEREIGN — you OWN your model; it lives on your machine; the AI
    everyone else builds is a model OF you that THEY own and can switch off.
  - LONG GAME: INTEROPERABLE — eventually your self can safely represent you to
    other people's agents and services. (Deliberately LAST — see risks.)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. WHY NOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. The betrayal is fresh and repeating. Rewind was acquired by Meta and its
   local-first app was killed (Dec 2025); Khoj shut its cloud (Apr 2026); vendors
   routinely deprecate or region-restrict the AI/memory people depend on. The
   through-line: the AI you rely on is not yours and can vanish. A wave of burned,
   privacy-minded, technical users is looking for a durable home right now.
2. The pain is at its peak. Native memory (ChatGPT, Claude) just gave 800M people
   a memory inside each tool — which is exactly when CROSS-TOOL fragmentation
   becomes the universal pain. Cursor forgets what Claude decided. Nobody solves
   cross-vendor + local + cited.
3. The moat formed for free. General "computer-use" autonomy proved unreliable in
   2026 (low task accuracy, unsolved prompt-injection). So durable value moved to
   the two things the big labs can't give an individual: a cited, owned model of
   who you are, and a per-action receipt that makes delegated work trustworthy.

THE WEDGE IS CONTINUITY, NOT PRIVACY. "Protect your data" never sells (concern
doesn't convert — it's what killed Solid over 8 years). "Own your digital self
before someone else does" DOES: it's about a thing you'll LOSE, not a thing you
should morally care about. Honest boundary: we protect the model of YOU, not the
frontier model underneath — so the claim is "the model of you they can't take,
portable across any AI," never "the AI they can't switch off."

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. WHO IT'S FOR FIRST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The AI-native builder — the founder/engineer living in Cursor + Claude Code +
ChatGPT all day, whose sharpest pain is losing architecture decisions and the
"why" to context compaction, and who already hand-maintains CLAUDE.md / session.md
as the workaround. Why them: they already live in MCP clients (60-second install,
zero behavior change); they ARE the burned, privacy-primed refugee cohort; their
work has built-in receipts (a draft is diffable, a PR reviewable) so agency can
safely START where verification is cheap; they already pay for AI tools; they're
our tribe. Honest sizing: thousands to seed, not millions. A beachhead, not the TAM.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. THE PRODUCT (dead simple)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE ONE EXPERIENCE: "It writes the reply, in your words. You press send."
A draft is already waiting in the tool you're in, written the way you would write
it, with one quiet line under it: "From 3 of your memories · here's what I refused
to assume · Edit · Send." That single moment IS the product. The memory, the
layers, the connectors, the tokens, the receipts — all invisible machinery.
The user never learns the words "embedding," "layer," "MCP," or "vault."

THE AGENCY TRUST LADDER (the self earns the right to do more, safely):
  Rung 0 — BRIEF (read-only, zero side effects): before you talk to someone or open
    a task, a cited "here's what to know" is already there. Nothing to approve;
    never touches the outside world.
  Rung 1 — DRAFT, YOU SEND (reversible, one tap): THE WEDGE. The self writes a real
    reply/comment/update in your voice and leaves it un-sent. You edit (one key) or
    send (one tap). If you edit, it quietly learns the correction. ***This is the
    permanent ceiling for the shippable product.***
  Rungs 2–4 — standing permission / multi-step / acting with others: labeled
    RESEARCH, not roadmap. We do NOT ship autonomous sending. The instant an AI
    acts without a per-action tap, the user (and we) inherit real legal liability.
    Irreversibility + autonomy must NEVER combine.

HOW SOVEREIGNTY IS FELT (not a settings page): a prominent "Export everything,
including from us" button; one-tap Undo on anything; a receipt under every action;
"it still works with the plug pulled." Ownership is shown, not preached.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. THE ONE FIRST BET
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Ship REAL LOCAL EMBEDDINGS (no API key) + THE MIRROR MOMENT.

Replace the keyword-hash "semantics" with a real, small, on-device model (e.g.
Model2Vec potion-base-8M or bge-small via ONNX). Then surface ONE thing the self
learned, in the user's own words, with its source: "You tend to decline meetings
before 10am — from your calendar, 6 times." Right, or correct-in-one-tap.

Why this first: it's safe (read-only, nothing leaves the machine), it makes the
sovereignty claim technically TRUE offline for the first time, it's the holy-shit
"it actually knows me" beat, and it's the credibility floor under every later
claim. Until it ships, "a model of you" is keyword-search-in-disguise, and no
"acts as me" / "semantic memory" claim should be made (that's AI-washing).

Reality check: this is real (small, ~1–2 day) engineering — including handling that
the local model outputs 256 dims vs our 384-dim index, and a background re-embed —
NOT a config flip. (An abandoned half-edit is sitting in the tree from an earlier
attempt; it should be redone properly with tests.)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. THE ROADMAP (condensed)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
P0  Real local embeddings + the mirror moment.            (THE FIRST BET; read-only)
P1  Falsifiable voice + self-correcting memory: a "sounds-like-me" test the build
    must pass, and automatic detection of contradictory/stale memories flagged for
    a one-tap human vouch (nobody else does this; append-only memory rots).
P2  Rung 0 shipped — the cited Brief inside your tools (read-only agency).
P3  Rung 1 shipped — DRAFT-AND-HOLD (THE WEDGE): a "draft in my voice, I press
    send" tool + the per-action RECEIPT, built on the existing permission + audit
    spine. Smallest possible write surface, reversible by construction.
P4  Rung 2 (RESEARCH) — one narrow, reversible, standing-permission chore, unlocked
    only after a proven track record; every act reversible with visible Undo.
P5  Rung 3 (RESEARCH) — a multi-step routine with a pre-defined rollback per step.
P6  Rung 4 / interop (LONG GAME, last) — your self hands a signed, scoped, cited
    "mandate" to another agent; needs real asymmetric signing first; never
    impersonation, always "acting on behalf of, disclosed."
Cross-cutting: notarized/auto-updating app; encryption at rest; refactor the
18k-line storage god-object; accounts + billing when value is proven.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. THE MOAT + THE ONE METRIC
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Moat (compounding, hard to copy): cross-vendor neutrality (the big labs won't
unify your Cursor + Claude + ChatGPT — it's against their lock-in); an owned,
local, inspectable model of you; and provable, controllable, BOUNDED action
(receipts + human approval) instead of chasing risky autonomy.

North-star metric: the "wrong-in-my-voice" incident rate — how often the self
produces something a user would NOT have sent. Drive it to near zero. Not
"hours saved" theater. Also track: does an external AI visibly use your context
(the real activation), draft-send-without-edit rate, and deletions/corrections
(trust in action).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. WHAT WE WILL NOT DO (safety + honesty guardrails)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- No autonomous send / publish / spend / delete. Ever. (Draft-and-hold is the cap.)
- Always-approve hard gates forever: external messages to new people, money,
  deletes, publishing, anything irreversible.
- No screen/audio "record everything" capture (the Rewind trap; a trust and legal
  catastrophe). Win on connectors, not surveillance.
- Never claim "it IS you" / a "digital twin." Always "works from you; you press send."
- Never say "semantic memory" until real local embeddings ship and beat the baseline.
- Receipts are a trust/control surface, NOT a legal shield — never market them as
  legal defensibility.
- Treat any draft influenced by external content (an email body, a web page) as
  possibly poisoned, and FLAG it in the receipt (prompt-injection is unsolved).
- Get E&O / product-liability insurance before issuing the first write-capable token.
- Keep it dead simple: no new tabs, no wall of toggles, no editor/notes UI. The
  surface must not grow as the capability grows.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10. MONEY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Free: the memory / model of you (never charge someone to own their own data).
~$20/mo: the SOVEREIGN self-layer — drafts + receipts + cross-tool sync (this is
where the willingness-to-pay premium lives). Later: a value-metered AGENCY tier as
users climb the trust ladder. Pricing must never betray the ownership promise.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
11. THE HONEST RISKS (so we don't fool ourselves)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- "A model of you" does nothing for you on day one — Personal.ai pitched almost
  exactly this for years with no breakout. We MUST prove single-player utility (a
  draft you actually send) fast, or inherit the same fate.
- "Own your data" as a standalone cause is a graveyard (Solid: 8 years right and
  dead). That's why interop is sequenced LAST, and why we lead with a working draft.
- "AI acting as a person" draws heavy regulatory scrutiny (the companion-app
  lawsuits). Anti-companion, "works and proves it, never bonds," is a survival
  constraint, not a nicety.
- The platforms can do this. Apple's on-device agent + 2B devices is the base-case
  threat. Our narrow, real edge is cross-vendor neutrality and local ownership —
  we must stay narrow and honest, not claim to be "the trust layer for humanity."
- The whole field's fatal failure is "a confident wrong answer in your voice."
  Nobody can teach a clone taste. So we do NOT compete on how autonomous/human-like
  it is — we compete on a deliberately BOUNDED action you approve, with a receipt.

THE FIRST THREE MOVES: (1) real local embeddings + the mirror moment; (2) the
"sounds-like-me" test + contradiction flags; (3) draft-and-hold with a receipt.
Make the memory real, make the value visible, let the self do bounded work — safely.
